# Presentation / marketing
Examples: product landing, portfolio, brand site, showroom. Define narrative and primary audience/CTA before visual style. Use strong composition, typography, purposeful imagery, editorial pacing and product-specific motifs. Display animation, material and scroll-linked storytelling may support meaning; keep essential information available without effects, avoid scroll hijacking and distracting loops. Verify legibility throughout transitions, page weight, responsive art direction, reduced motion, content hierarchy and actual links. Static hosting describes delivery, not whether animation exists.
