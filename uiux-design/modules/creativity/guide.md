# Creative intelligence
Start with the problem and constraints, not a template. Generate genuinely different alternatives by changing composition, semantic encoding, geometry, hierarchy, material, interaction model or temporal choreography. Include existing and no-change/no-motion options. Low importance: choose a justified option; medium 2–3 candidates; high 3–5; signature deeper as budget permits. Compare readability, task fit, originality, continuity, accessibility, technical cost and maintenance. Combine complementary strengths only if coherent.
Anti-generic review: ask what product-specific information or visual motif drives the design; remove arbitrary decoration, duplicated chrome and gratuitous cards. Distinctiveness may be quiet; do not force novel shapes for familiar controls. Study real examples for underlying principles; don't copy proprietary assets or hallucinate their design. Produce isolated rendered prototypes for important alternatives; record why rejected and why selected. User feedback updates project preferences, not universal rules.
